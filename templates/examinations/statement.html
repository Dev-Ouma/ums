Grading Regulations & Senate Computation Standards

All published course grades conform to the Kenyan Commission for University Education (CUE) standards and institutional examination bylaws. Semester Grade Point Average (GPA) is weighted by course unit credit values. Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) represents the cumulative performance across all completed sessions. For official registry verification or formal transcripts, please use the Official Transcript export.

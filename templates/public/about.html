Our Heritage & Foundation

Born from 4-Star Commercial Hospitality

Wigot Hotel commenced operations in 2014 and quickly established an unmatched reputation as a premier business and family leisure destination in Western Kenya. Situated approximately four kilometers from Kisumu's Central Business District against the panoramic backdrop of Kajulu Hills, the hotel welcomed thousands of corporate and international guests.

Drawing from years of front-line experience in the hospitality landscape, Wigot noticed a critical gap in the training and development of current and future hospitality professionals. Traditional classrooms lacked real-world hotel pressure, commercial culinary kitchen speed, and five-star guest etiquette.

In 2021, this insight led to the birth of Wigot School of Hospitality — an accredited vocational college where students learn directly inside operating commercial kitchens, bakeries, cocktail lounges, and hotel suites under executive chef mentorship.

How Window Controls & Security Gates Operate:
  • Master Access Switch: If toggled OFF, student downloads are instantly locked regardless of dates.
  • Automated Date Windows: If an Opening Date or Locking Date is specified, documents automatically open and lock at the designated timestamp.
  • Financial Clearance Gate: When enabled, students with outstanding fee balances exceeding the Max Allowed Balance are barred from downloading the document and redirected to the payment clearance portal.
  • Registry Bypass: System administrators and registry personnel bypass download gates for audit and dispatch purposes.
